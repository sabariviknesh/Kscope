About Kaleidoscope Consulting

12 years of selling. Built into a system.

Sales consulting, coaching and execution for founders and growing B2B businesses — led by someone who has actually carried a bag and a number.

Sabariviknesh Nagarajan

Founder, Kaleidoscope Consulting

12 years in B2B sales

"I started as a field salesman selling large RO water treatment plants — walking into construction sites and pitching contractors on installing plants for new buildings. I had no idea what I was doing."

I lost clients constantly — not because the product was bad, but because I didn't know how to qualify someone, find the right person to talk to, or handle it when they pushed back. When a conversation went well, I honestly didn't know how to close it.

Over the next twelve years I sold across e-commerce, printing, food, and pharma, and eventually into IT — software, custom development, AI, cybersecurity, and data governance — to clients across Singapore, Australia, Europe, and the US.

Every mistake I made selling RO plants, I made again selling enterprise software. Just at a bigger scale. Eventually I stopped making them — and Kaleidoscope Consulting is that system, built for other founders and teams so they don't have to learn it the same expensive way.
